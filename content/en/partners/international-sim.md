## Internet access during an international trip

Mobile data can help you check maps, look up addresses, review reservations, and stay in touch while abroad. Before choosing a plan, check its coverage for your destination, device compatibility, data allowance, and validity directly with the provider.

The promotional campaign previously mentioned on this site has not been confirmed. Do not assume that a discount is currently available; check the provider's current terms before purchasing.
