Planning an international trip also means thinking about how you will stay connected after you arrive. Internet access abroad can help you check maps, look up addresses, review reservations, and let family know you are doing well.

## What is Labor Day in the United States?

Labor Day is a US federal holiday recognizing the contributions of workers. It is observed on the first Monday in September. In 2026, it fell on September 7, and the following Tuesday was September 8.

## Staying connected while traveling

An international travel SIM can make everyday tasks easier, such as finding an address on a map, checking opening hours, looking up a reservation, or keeping in touch with people at home. Before you leave, save important details for offline access and check where your itinerary may have limited coverage.
